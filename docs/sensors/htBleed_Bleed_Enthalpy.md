# Sensor htBleed: Bleed Enthalpy

- **Unit:** BTU/lb
- **Physical Domain:** Environmental control system (ECS) bleed port
- **Nominal Operating Range:** 390.00 to 395.00 BTU/lb
- **Degradation Dynamics:** Thermal efficiency marker for customer bleed air extraction.
- **Failure Modes:** Bleed air pre-cooler valve leakage.
