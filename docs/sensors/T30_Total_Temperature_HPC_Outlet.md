# Sensor T30: Total Temperature at HPC Outlet

- **Unit:** Degrees Rankine (°R)
- **Physical Domain:** High Pressure Compressor (HPC) discharge / Combustor inlet
- **Nominal Operating Range:** 1580.00 °R to 1600.00 °R
- **Degradation Dynamics:** Direct primary indicator of HPC blade tip clearance erosion.
- **Failure Modes:** Combustor over-temperature and thermal fatigue.
