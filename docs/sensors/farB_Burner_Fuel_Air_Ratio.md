# Sensor farB: Burner Fuel-Air Ratio

- **Unit:** Dimensionless stoichiometric ratio
- **Physical Domain:** Main combustor combustion chamber
- **Nominal Operating Range:** 0.030 to 0.032
- **Degradation Dynamics:** Stoichiometric shift resulting from air bleed leakage.
- **Failure Modes:** Hot section thermal hotspots and emissions rise.
