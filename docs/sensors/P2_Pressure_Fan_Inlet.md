# Sensor P2: Total Pressure at Fan Inlet

- **Unit:** psia (pounds per square inch absolute)
- **Physical Domain:** Engine nacelle aerodynamic intake
- **Nominal Operating Range:** 14.62 psia baseline (sea level static)
- **Degradation Dynamics:** Tracks flight envelope altitude and Mach variation.
- **Failure Modes:** Pitot-static tube blockage and pressure transducer calibration bias.
