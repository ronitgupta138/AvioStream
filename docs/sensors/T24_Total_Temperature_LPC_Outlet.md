# Sensor T24: Total Temperature at LPC Outlet

- **Unit:** Degrees Rankine (°R)
- **Physical Domain:** Low Pressure Compressor (LPC) discharge
- **Nominal Operating Range:** 641.50 °R to 643.50 °R
- **Degradation Dynamics:** Rises gradually as LPC blade fouling reduces compression efficiency.
- **Failure Modes:** Thermal boundary layer separation and aerodynamic stall.
