# Sensor Nf: Physical Fan Rotor Speed

- **Unit:** RPM (revolutions per minute)
- **Physical Domain:** Low-pressure spool shaft
- **Nominal Operating Range:** 2387.00 RPM to 2389.00 RPM
- **Degradation Dynamics:** Mechanical vibration and shaft eccentricity tracking.
- **Failure Modes:** Fan blade foreign object damage (FOD) and bearing wear.
