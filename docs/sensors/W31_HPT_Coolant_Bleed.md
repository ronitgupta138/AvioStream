# Sensor W31: High Pressure Turbine Coolant Bleed

- **Unit:** lbm/s
- **Physical Domain:** Turbine cooling circuit
- **Nominal Operating Range:** 38.50 to 39.00 lbm/s
- **Degradation Dynamics:** Blade cooling passage oxidation and film cooling blockage.
- **Failure Modes:** High pressure turbine blade thermal ablation.
