# Sensor T50: Total Temperature at LPT Outlet

- **Unit:** Degrees Rankine (°R)
- **Physical Domain:** Low Pressure Turbine (LPT) exhaust
- **Nominal Operating Range:** 1400.00 °R to 1420.00 °R
- **Degradation Dynamics:** Exhaust gas temperature (EGT) margin deterioration indicator.
- **Failure Modes:** Turbine stator vane thermal oxidation.
