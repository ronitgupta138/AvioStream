# EGT Exhaust Gas Temperature Margin

## Overview
Tracks thermal degradation and turbine health margin against redline limits.

## Physics & Degradation Mechanics
As turbine blades erode, required combustor temperatures rise to maintain thrust.

## Thresholds & Telemetry Alerting
- Nominal: > 25°C margin
- Caution: 10°C - 25°C
- Critical: < 10°C
