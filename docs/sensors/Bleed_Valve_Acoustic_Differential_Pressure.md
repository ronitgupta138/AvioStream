# Bleed Valve Ultrasonic Acoustic & Differential Pressure Telemetry

## 1. Operating Mechanics
Transient bleed valves (TBV) regulate compressor stall margins during rapid engine deceleration. High-frequency acoustic emission probes detect seal leakage prior to pneumatic failure.

## 2. Frequency Domain Signature
- **Nominal Flow:** Broadband aerodynamic hiss between $10\,\text{kHz}$ and $25\,\text{kHz}$.
- **Turbulent Leakage:** Prominent ultrasonic harmonic resonance peaks between $38\,\text{kHz}$ and $44\,\text{kHz}$.

## 3. Differential Pressure Dynamic Ratio
$$\Delta P = P_{\text{inlet}} - P_{\text{discharge}}$$
Monitored via dual piezoresistive differential pressure transducers to verify valve seating closure under cruise throttle regimes.
