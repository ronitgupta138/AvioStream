# Turbine Thermal Stress & Creep Dynamics

## 1. Larson-Miller Creep Parameter
High pressure turbine blades operate above 1400°C under continuous centrifugal stress. Creep rupture life follows the Larson-Miller parameter $P = T(\log t_r + C)$.

## 2. Telemetry Indicators
- Exhaust gas temperature T50 rise
- Increased coolant bleed requirement W31
- High temperature dwell time tracking.
