# Fan Blade Aerodynamic Erosion

## 1. Mechanisms
Erosion occurs along the outer 30% of the titanium fan blade span due to sand, dust, and rain droplet collisions. This alters the airfoil camber, reducing total pressure ratio.

## 2. Telemetry Signature
- Drop in P15 bypass pressure
- Minor rise in corrected fan speed NRf
- Slight reduction in overall engine thrust margin.
