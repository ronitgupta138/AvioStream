# High-Pressure Turbine Nozzle Guide Vane Oxidation Kinetics Specification

## 1. Environmental Degradation Mechanics
Nozzle guide vanes endure continuous exposure to combustor discharge gases exceeding $1400^\circ\text{C}$, causing high-temperature oxidation of protective MCrAlY overlay coatings.

## 2. Parabolic Oxidation Rate Law
Oxide scale thickness growth $x$ obeys Wagner's parabolic rate equation:

$$x^2 = k_p \cdot t$$

Where parabolic rate constant $k_p$ exhibits Arrhenius temperature dependence:
$$k_p = k_0 \exp\left(-\frac{Q}{R T_g}\right)$$

## 3. Coating Depletion Warning
When calculated oxide thickness exceeds critical threshold ($x > 80\,\mu\text{m}$), thermal barrier spallation risk escalates, prompting scheduled borescope inspection flags.
