# High Pressure Turbine (HPT) Thermal Creep Damage Modeling

## 1. Larson-Miller Parameterization
$$P_{LM} = T \cdot (C + \log_{10} t_r)$$
- Models progressive structural deformation of single-crystal turbine blades operating above $1200^{\circ}\text{C}$.
- Computes cumulative mechanical fatigue and remaining thermal shock margins.
